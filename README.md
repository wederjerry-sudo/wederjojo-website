Wederjojo (@wederjojo) — Multilingual Prototype

«ሰው መሆን ይቀድማል — Being Human Comes First.»

A multilingual personal website focused on humanity, learning, personal growth, mentorship, and community empowerment.

Overview

Wederjojo is a digital space created to share:

- Inspirational reflections and practical life skills.
- Educational stories and creative works.
- Resources for women, parents, and children.
- Mentorship and community development.

Languages

- Amharic
- English
- Arabic

Core Values

- Humanity
- Learning and Growth
- Empowerment
- Life and Purpose
- Mentorship
- Faith and Values
- Community and Impact

Project Status

Prototype in active development.
